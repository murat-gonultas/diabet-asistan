# Diabet Asistan - Product Vision

## 1. Product Summary

Diabet Asistan is a safety-first carbohydrate logging assistant for children with Type 1 diabetes and their parents.

The product helps families document meals, estimate carbohydrate amounts, review and correct entries, and build a family-specific recipe memory. Later versions may add AI-assisted photo-based carbohydrate estimation and Dexcom-based post-meal glucose analysis.

## 2. Core Product Principle

Diabet Asistan is a carbohydrate logging and review assistant, not insulin dosing software.

The application must never calculate insulin doses, control insulin pumps, trigger automatic bolus delivery, or replace medical treatment decisions.

## 3. Target Users

### Child User

- Creates meal records.
- Adds a meal photo.
- Enters estimated carbohydrates manually.
- Selects saved family recipes.
- Views meal history.

### Parent User

- Reviews all child meal records.
- Approves or corrects carbohydrate values.
- Creates and manages family recipes.
- Monitors meal history and correction patterns.

### Future Clinical / Research User

Not part of MVP v0.1. Later versions may support read-only reporting for clinicians, diabetes educators, or research workflows.

## 4. Problem Statement

Children with Type 1 diabetes and their parents need a practical way to document meals and estimate carbohydrates consistently. Mixed meals, home-cooked food, cultural dishes, and portion uncertainty make carbohydrate estimation difficult.

The first product goal is not to automate therapy. The first goal is to create a structured, family-controlled meal and carbohydrate review workflow.

## 5. MVP Value Proposition

Diabet Asistan v0.1 helps a family answer:

- What did the child eat?
- When was the meal?
- What carbohydrate amount was estimated?
- Did a parent approve or correct it?
- What final carbohydrate value was used for documentation?
- Is this a repeated family meal or recipe?

## 6. Long-Term Vision

The long-term product can evolve into a personalized meal intelligence platform for families managing Type 1 diabetes:

1. Manual carbohydrate logging.
2. Parent review and correction.
3. Family recipe memory.
4. Photo-based AI meal recognition.
5. Confidence-aware carbohydrate range estimation.
6. Dexcom OAuth integration.
7. Post-meal glucose response analysis.
8. Research-ready pseudonymized data exports.

## 7. Non-Goals

The following are explicit non-goals:

- Insulin dose calculation.
- Insulin pump control.
- Automatic bolus delivery.
- Medical diagnosis.
- Medical treatment recommendation.
- Replacement of diabetes care professionals.

## 8. Product Positioning

Diabet Asistan should be positioned as:

> A family-controlled carbohydrate logging and review assistant for children with Type 1 diabetes.

It should not be positioned as:

> An insulin calculator, diabetes treatment engine, or automated therapy system.
